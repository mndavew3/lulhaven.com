🎃 Give Haven back its head

Every router you have ever owned is a headless device — the industry's own word for a machine with no screen, no face, nothing to look you in the eye. Ichabod Crane would understand. Your Haven deserves more.

The only one. No other router on the planet ships with a full-color display and an input device. Plug in the screen and Haven grows a head: live traffic, blocked threats, a speed test, a gamer's lag report, even the weather — right on the router, no app, no login, no laptop.

LIVE DEMO — LANDING SHORTLY

This is not going to be a video or a mockup. The router's actual display firmware, compiled for your browser — wheel through the screens, click the buttons, run the speed test, try the shuffled keypad.

What the head shows

  • The home face — internet status, live traffic graph, blocks today, headroom.
  • Network — traffic detail, a real speed test, every device, port lights, internet health.
  • Special interests — a gamer's ping/jitter/bufferbloat report card, the weather, a guest-WiFi sign for your shop counter, and the router's own honest footprint audit.
  • The owner's ring — sensitive screens locked behind a one-time code your phone reads from the sign-in page, entered on a keypad that shuffles itself every time.

And it costs the router almost nothing

The entire display program is a tenth of one megabyte — smaller than a single photo — and it draws at the lowest priority the router has. Filtering always wins. The screen starves first. That's a promise written into the code.

With a Haven, you just look. That’s the whole instruction manual.
