Screen Haven

A free, open status display for your OpenWrt router. Live bandwidth, system health, network and connected devices — on the inexpensive 3.5-inch screen, driven by a USB mouse you plug into the router. A glimpse of what a Haven does.

Open-source · MIT · coming to the OpenWrt feed

The screens

Your whole router, at a glance

What it is

Beautiful, and only yours

Screen Haven turns the little USB display into a live dashboard for your router. It reads only your own network's state — bandwidth, load, memory, connected devices, Wi-Fi — and shows it right on the device. No app, no cloud, no account, nothing sent anywhere. It runs on any OpenWrt box with the 3.5-inch screen and a USB mouse (or a wireless air-mouse) attached. Most routers have only one USB port, so a small hub is what lets the two share it. You turn the mouse wheel to move between screens and click to act.

For reviewers and creators

Press the wheel. Get a clean screenshot.

Every screen here can be captured from the device itself. Push the mouse wheel and Screen Haven writes what you are looking at as a 480×320 PNG — the clean frame, with no cursor drawn into it — ready to publish as it is. No capture card, no phone photograph of a glowing panel, no cropping afterwards.

Each capture is saved twice: onto the router, where you can view and download it from the web admin, and — if a USB stick is plugged in — straight onto the stick in a Haven-Screenshots folder. Pull the stick out and the images are on your desktop. The copy held on the router lives in memory, so leave a stick in it if you are capturing a lot.

All of this runs on about $30 of hardware — the 3.5-inch screen and a small USB hub — on a router you already own. Most routers have a single USB port, and the hub is what lets the screen and a mouse share it. Any USB or wireless mouse will do. The software is free.

A taste of Haven

Screen Haven is the free, open little sibling of Haven — the content-filtering router that gives you simple control over what's on your network, for homes, schools and offices alike. Like what you see here? The whole product goes much further.
