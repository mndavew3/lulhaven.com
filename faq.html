Questions people ask

How do I block adult content on my whole-home Wi-Fi?

Haven filters at the router, so one rule covers every device in the house at once. Adult content is one of about 50 categories — it's off by default, and you switch it on in the Haven Helm, the on-router control panel. Haven is a content filter, not a single-purpose porn blocker: you choose what to filter, and adult content is simply one of the things you can turn on.

Can someone get around it with a VPN or encrypted DNS?

No — that's the difference. The workarounds that defeat ordinary filters don't work here: consumer VPN apps, encrypted DNS (DoH) built into browsers, Apple Private Relay, and manually changing a device's DNS are all caught and routed back through Haven. The filter holds even when someone in the home actively tries to slip past it.

Can I buy a router that already has the filtering set up?

Yes. Haven ships pre-flashed — you plug it in and it works. There's no firmware to install, no command line, and no per-device setup. It ships filtering nothing, so you start from a clean slate and turn on exactly what your household wants.

Does it work without installing an app on every device?

Yes. Because Haven filters at the network level, there's nothing to install on phones, tablets, laptops, or TVs — including iPhones and other devices you can't normally lock down. Anything that joins your Wi-Fi is filtered automatically.

Does Haven see my browsing or keep my data?

No. Your filter settings, your device list, and the activity log live on your router and never leave it. The company that makes Haven cannot read what flows through your network. The only outbound contact is a daily check for updated category lists, and it carries nothing that identifies you.

Does the filtering follow a phone off my Wi-Fi?

It can. For phones that leave the house — on cellular or other Wi-Fi — Haven offers off-network filtering through a Private DNS setting, so the same household rules keep applying wherever the device goes. No app required.

Is Haven only for families with kids?

No. Haven filters for whoever is in the home — a parent setting household rules, an adult choosing to cut rage-bait and algorithmic feeds out of their own day, or a guest on your Wi-Fi. Family use is one reason people buy it, not the only one.

How is this different from my router's built-in parental controls?

Most built-in parental controls are easy to bypass — a VPN app or an encrypted-DNS toggle defeats them in seconds. Haven is built to close those workarounds, keeps your data on your own router instead of a company cloud, and ships ready to use with about 50 categories you control.

Why is Haven WiFi 6 and not WiFi 7?

Because in everyday use you won't feel the difference, and WiFi 6 is the proven, broadly-supported choice — past the bleeding edge. WiFi 7 only does anything when both your router and your device are WiFi 7, and nearly everything in homes today is WiFi 6 or older — so most WiFi 7 buyers are paying for it and running WiFi 6 anyway. The real-world gain is a second here and there on things you'd plug in for. Want WiFi 7 regardless? Run Haven on your own WiFi 7 router with Gold.
