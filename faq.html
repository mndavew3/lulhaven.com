Questions people ask

How do I block adult content on my whole-home Wi-Fi?

Haven filters at the router, so one rule covers every device on your network at once. Adult content is one of 49 categories — it's off by default, and you switch it on in the Haven Helm, the on-router control panel. Haven is a content filter, not a single-purpose porn blocker: you choose what to filter, and adult content is simply one of the things you can turn on.

Can someone get around it with a VPN or encrypted DNS?

The workarounds that defeat ordinary filters are covered here: consumer VPN apps, encrypted DNS (DoH) built into browsers, Apple Private Relay, and manually changing a device's DNS are all caught and routed back through Haven. That's the difference — most filters stop at DNS, and DNS alone is easy to step around.

Can I buy a router that already has the filtering set up?

Yes. Haven ships pre-flashed — you plug it in and it works. There's no firmware to install, no command line, and no per-device setup. It ships filtering nothing, so you start from a clean slate and turn on exactly what you want.

Does it work without installing an app on every device?

Yes. Because Haven filters at the network level, there's nothing to install on phones, tablets, laptops, or TVs — including iPhones and other devices you can't normally lock down. Anything that joins your Wi-Fi is filtered automatically.

Does Haven see my browsing or keep my data?

No. Your filter settings, your device list, and the activity log live on your router and never leave it. The company that makes Haven cannot read what flows through your network. The only outbound contact is a daily check for updated category lists, and it carries nothing that identifies you.

Does the filtering follow a phone off my Wi-Fi?

It can. For phones that leave your network — on cellular or other Wi-Fi — Haven offers off-network filtering through a Private DNS setting, so the same rules keep applying wherever the device goes. No app required.

Is Haven only for families with kids?

No. Haven filters for whoever is on your network — a parent setting the rules, an adult choosing to cut rage-bait and algorithmic feeds out of their own day, a guest on your Wi-Fi, or a small office or care home setting one policy for everyone. Family use is one reason people buy it, not the only one.

How is this different from my router's built-in parental controls?

Most built-in parental controls are easy to bypass — a VPN app or an encrypted-DNS toggle defeats them in seconds. Haven is built to close those workarounds, keeps your data on your own router instead of a company cloud, and ships ready to use with 49 categories you control.

Why is Haven WiFi 6 and not WiFi 7?

Haven Navy covers every WiFi 6 device you already own — which is most things you already own today. WiFi 7 only delivers a real benefit when both your router and your device are WiFi 7, and almost nothing in use today meets both requirements. If you specifically need WiFi 7, Haven Gold runs on your own hardware, including any WiFi 7 router you already have.

How is Haven different from a cloud DNS filter like NextDNS?

Cloud DNS filters route your lookups through their servers — which means your query history lives in their cloud, not yours. NextDNS logs this by design and offers it as a feature (query analytics). Haven holds everything on your router: settings, device list, activity log. Nothing reaches our servers except a daily version check carrying no identifying information.

Haven also operates at the network layer, not just DNS. VPN tunnels, encrypted DNS (DoH), and Apple Private Relay — bypass methods that cloud filters cannot see — are caught and blocked at the router before they leave your network. And because Haven runs on hardware on your own network, it works whether our servers are up or not.

What happens if I end my subscription?

Your filter keeps working. The subscription funds monthly list updates — new domains added, categories refined, new bypass tactics closed. If you cancel, your router keeps filtering everything you had configured, using the last list it received. It just stops receiving updates. Most competitors turn filtering off the day you cancel. Haven doesn't.

Will Haven slow down my internet?

No. Haven runs on hardware built for multi-gigabit throughput. The filtering adds no perceptible latency to everyday use — streaming, gaming, video calls, and large downloads all work at full speed.
