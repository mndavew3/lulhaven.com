## How it works

- **The Helm — where you steer** — web-based; reach it from any device on your Wi-Fi at haven.lan
- **Search across categories and providers**
- **Two modes** — Simple mode for everyday use, Advanced when you want to tinker
- **Activity log** — see what is being blocked
- **Always-allow list** — sites you mark are never blocked, no matter the category
- **Per-provider settings** — tune anything individually
- **Plug in, set a password, you are filtering** — detects your existing network and avoids conflicts
- **Everything your old router did, Haven does too** — Haven adds, never subtracts
- **$4/month for category updates** — below the typical $70–$100/year competitor range
- **Haven Navy** — Wi-Fi 6, dual 2.5GbE, our primary recommendation
- **Haven Olive** — Wi-Fi 5, secondary
- **Interactive demo at lulhaven.com/demo** — no signup

## What Haven does not do

- **We block at the network level** — we can stop you reaching bad sites but never read what you do on the good ones
