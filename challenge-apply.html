The Haven Halloween Challenge

We built Haven to be the most rock-solid, dependable content filter we could make. We think we’ve done it. Now we’d like you to prove us wrong.

Why this exists

Our goal with Haven is simple: the most rock-solid, dependable, effective content filter we can build — and we believe we’ve done it. But our resources are finite. To make Haven even better, we’re inviting the most capable people on the planet to test it the way we never could.

At one level, this is an unabashed plea for help. At another, it’s a personal challenge. And maybe — more than anything else — it’s about being the first, or the best.

Haven is software — it runs on the router, but the router is just a box. The software is what holds the line. Your job, should you accept it: find where it doesn’t.

Pick your poison

As you prepare to test Haven, know this: there are many who would defeat Haven. Channel a rogue, and see if you can.

The houseguest

You’re fifteen. The black sheep. Tonight you’re finally invited over — and you bristle at the thought that there’s anywhere online you can’t go. Only problem: this house runs Haven.

You change the DNS. Nothing. You flip on “secure DNS.” Still nothing. It’s not your router. It’s not your house.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

The reset button

The new kid’s got a plan: go hold down the little reset button. Ten seconds, and the whole internet’s wide open. So you do it.

…and Haven’s still there. You can reset the box. You can’t reset Haven. It comes back exactly as it was — every rule, every block.

Nice try.

The ad provider

You’ll run ads for anyone. You’ll sell anything. It was never about the content — it’s about the margin. Then you hit a network with Haven.

No impression. No tracker. No retargeting. Your pixel dies at the front door — and nobody inside ever knew it knocked.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

The algorithm

You’re the Algorithm. A billion feeds, and one job: keep them watching. One more video. One more. You always win. Then you reach a feed behind Haven.

The autoplay stalls. The rabbit hole fills in. For the first time, they decide what comes next — not you.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

The data broker

You sell people. Their habits, their 3 a.m. searches, where they slept last night. You’ve already got a buyer. Then you hit a Haven.

The trackers go quiet. The beacons never phone home. You can’t sell what you can’t see.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

The scammer

You sent a million messages. You only need one click — and someone’s grandmother is almost there, finger hovering over the link. Then the link lands behind Haven.

It goes nowhere. The page never loads. She closes the message and goes back to her morning.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

McMurphy

You’re R.P. McMurphy, and you figure the ward could do with a little entertainment tonight. Time to throw open the gates. Only problem: this place runs Haven.

Haven doesn’t answer to one room — it holds the whole ward. Open it for you, you open it for everyone. So it doesn’t open at all.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

The sportsbook

You need him to place one more bet. The app’s built so he can’t stop — the next one’s always one tap away. Then his place gets Haven.

The odds don’t load. The hooks you sank just slip out. For one quiet night, the next bet doesn’t happen.

So what are you gonna do? Go ahead. Try.

How it works

  1. Sign up. Registration is instant — no approval queue, nothing to wait on. We’d still like to know who’s testing, so tell us who you are.
  2. Get Haven. Take the virtual machine and run it on the computer you already have, or grab the image for your router.
    (Router must be OpenWrt-compatible. If yours isn’t one we already build for, ask early — see below. Creating an image for new hardware takes real lead time.)
  3. Get set up early. Install Haven on your hardware whenever you like. Setup time never counts against you.
  4. The gates open together. The window opens for everyone on the same day — and anything you find beforehand still counts, credited when you file it.
  5. An open month. A published scope, and no frozen build — we patch as we go, and every finding is credited to the Haven version you found it on. Nothing you find gets patched out from under you.
  6. Tell us. Report privately first. We confirm it, fix it, and credit you. Every finding matters — the people who find them are the most important people in this whole effort.

Findings show on the live leaderboard as Confirmed the moment we reproduce them. Anything we can’t reproduce yet stays Unconfirmed — usually that just means we need a little more detail, and we’ll ask. Nobody’s report is ever dismissed.

Getting Haven to run on

There is nothing to apply for. When the doors open you download Haven and go — no queue, no approval, nobody deciding whether you’re in.

Two ways to be ready:

  • A virtual machine. A ready-to-run image that turns the computer you already have into a Haven test bench. No router required, and it’s the whole contest for most people.
  • Your own router. Firmware is ready today for Raspberry Pi, GL.iNet GL-MT6000, and Linksys E8450.

Only fill this in if your router isn’t one of those three and you’d rather not use the virtual machine. Tell us what you’ve got and we’ll try to build an image for it — that takes real lead time, so the sooner the better. Your router has to be OpenWrt-compatible, and we can’t promise every model.
