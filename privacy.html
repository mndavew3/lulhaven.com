Privacy

What this website collects, why, and who else sees it.

Haven is a privacy product, so here is plainly what lulhaven.com collects, why, and who else sees it. Last updated 2 October 2026.

What we collect

  • When you visit: the pages you view and what you click (buttons and links — never what you type into a box), your approximate location (country, region, city), your internet provider, and whether you're on a phone or a computer. Your browser keeps a random ID so we can tell repeat visits apart. It isn't tied to your name.
  • When you join the Challenge: a username, your email address and a password. Your password is stored scrambled, so nobody — including us — can read it. If you turn on an authenticator app, we store the secret it uses to make your codes.
  • When you submit a finding: its title and description, the settings file you export from your Haven, and any attachment.
  • When you apply, answer our survey, or sign up for news: what you enter in those forms, such as your name, email, handle and router.
  • Your internet address is recorded when you sign up, sign in, apply or submit a finding.

Why

To run the Challenge (accounts, judging, prizes), to prevent cheating and abuse, to email you about your account, findings and prizes, and to see how people use the site so we can improve it.

Who sees it

  • Judges see your findings without your name or email.
  • The public sees the usernames at the top of the leaderboard. Finders may be credited by username in our release notes.
  • Companies we use: Cloudflare runs the website and sees all traffic to it. Resend delivers our emails. Stripe handles payment if you buy a subscription.
  • No advertising trackers: no Google Analytics, no ad networks. We don't sell or rent your information.
  • Prize winners: after the Challenge closes, we'll email to ask where to send your plaque or router. You can say no.

Age

Challenge accounts are for people 13 and older. Receiving a prize requires being 18 or older, as the Challenge rules say.

How long

We keep these records. You can stop our news emails at any time with the unsubscribe link.

Cookies

Signing in sets a cookie that keeps you signed in (up to six hours for the Challenge). We don't use cookies for advertising.
