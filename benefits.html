Benefits

Haven is for intentional internet use — for people who decide what comes into their space rather than letting an ad algorithm, a gambling platform, a rage-bait publisher, or anything else you didn't invite decide for them. And for the makers who'd rather build that control themselves than rent it. Here's the why behind every feature.

And every one of them adds up to a single culminating benefit: your day feels a little more sane.

Why Haven

The others are better than nothing. Haven is another level of better.
Better filtering

Haven's curated blocklist draws on multiple sources across 47 categories — adult content, gambling, violence, misinformation, trackers, mobile game ads, and more. Millions of domains. Continuously updated.

Every category you turn on triggers up to 42 distinct strategies — not one, not three. Stacked layers that close the bypass loopholes others don't even know about, across all 613 providers.

Unlike Circle's preset profiles or your router's on/off switch, Haven's 47 categories are visible, editable, and explained — your values, your rules.

Easier to use

Why ease of use matters: in some cases, roughly two-thirds of the protectable devices go unprotected. — Family Online Safety Institute, 2025 (only 35% of game consoles that have protection options actually have protections enabled).

Browse the categories. Tap what you don't want. Save. That's the whole interface.

You see exactly what's blocked, why, and you can change anything in one click. Your values, your rules — not a vendor's opaque categories.

Set it up once for your entire network — and if you need to set it by device, you can do that too.

Built for you, priced for you

One Haven device protects every phone, tablet, TV, console, and IoT gadget on your network — no per-device installs, no per-child profiles, no per-app subscriptions.

The subscription is a low monthly fee for the latest category updates. Your filter keeps working whether you renew or not — it just stops getting new list updates. Most competitors charge several times as much, and when their payments stop, your filtering stops.

Privacy by architecture: the only thing Haven sends out is a version check. No query logs, no device identifiers, nothing linking the request to you. Cloud-based DNS filters like NextDNS log your traffic to their servers by design. Haven cannot — your data never leaves the box.
