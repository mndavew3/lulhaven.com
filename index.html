Haven
Declare Your Values

Haven is a content filter. Haven is for anyone who wants more control over what flows through their network. It could be valuable in several different contexts. For example, if you manage a nursing home, filtering violent content is a real mental health benefit for residents.

Haven was designed with a specific purpose: to help people express their values. If you’ve got children in your home and you filter porn from your network, that’s an expression of your values.

To filter something with Haven, you select a category. Then you see content providers to filter. When you select one of those content providers, Haven will use as many as 47 unique strategies to block that content from your Local Area Network.

Click "Save" and you are finished. Everyone you know could do this. Cool

Our single overriding goal in Haven is ease of use. So no matter what your values are, you can choose a profile that reflects them.

We maintain a live demo on our website. Haven is launching on Kickstarter — visit lulhaven.com/demo to get an early look.

How It Works

Haven is an add-on to the OpenWRT router software. Many routers come directly from the manufacturer with OpenWRT as their software. OpenWRT can replace the original software in millions of other routers. OpenWRT is the leading open-source router software available.

If you choose to filter nothing with Haven, then you’re just running unaltered OpenWRT, and that’s fine. Haven adds its own interface on top of OpenWRT, but all original OpenWRT screens and settings remain fully accessible.

Many content providers use a large number of IP addresses to provide their content. And that’s just one of about a dozen attributes that are used to filter content. And each attribute can be used in multiple strategies. With a single mouse click, you activate all those strategies.
