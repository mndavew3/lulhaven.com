Why Haven

Parental controls, privacy, or less algorithm?

Why not all three? Haven gives you the whole package for your entire network.

Parental controls

Block what shouldn't reach your network — on every device. They can't slip past it with a VPN, encrypted DNS, or Private Relay.

Privacy

Your settings, device list, and activity log stay on your router — never a company's cloud. Haven can't access your data even if it wanted to.

Less algorithm

With U-Haven, loosen the feed's grip on YouTube — for you, not just the kids.
