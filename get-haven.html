Before you download — read this once

  • You are choosing to modify your own equipment. The process runs on your equipment, in your hands, start to finish. So, we offer no guarantee of outcome.
  • The outcome — good or bad — is yours. Flashing firmware can make a device unusable if instructions aren’t followed or if the equipment does not match the image.
  • The archive contains firmware, step-by-step instructions, and possibly, helper scripts.
  • Nothing in this process contacts us or identifies you. You enter the Challenge only when you file your first finding.
  • Our humble suggestion is to back up your original firmware before you start and practice on expendable equipment until you are confident in the process.
