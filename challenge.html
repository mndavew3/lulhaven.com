The Haven Challenge

We built Haven to be the most rock-solid, dependable content filter we could make. We think we’ve done it. Soon, we’ll ask you to prove us wrong.

Attention: We have images ready for only a limited number of routers. If you want in and your router isn’t on the list, tell us quickly — we need lead time to prepare an image for your OpenWrt 24.10.4–compatible hardware.

What’s coming

Haven is software — it is hosted on your network. And Haven stands in the breach. The Challenge is an open invitation to the most capable people on the planet to find where it doesn’t.

One frozen build. A published scope. An open window. Find what we missed — and get credit for it.

Leaderboards, plural. An overall board, and one for each class of adversary — so there’s more than one way to claim first blood, and more than one board to top. Every confirmed finding is credited to the name that found it.

It’s not live yet. But the list is open. Get on it, and we’ll tell you the moment the gates open.
