    About the factory reset

    Haven is designed such that a factory reset does not disable your filter settings. A factory reset puts your Haven password and settings back to what they already were, not a generic default. If it's your password you've actually forgotten, resetting alone won't help — right after a reset you're offered a choice of ways to prove it's you (an authenticator app or an emailed code) so you can set a new one without losing anything else.

    A separate "Erase & hand off" button does what the factory reset used to do.

    How this works on a real Haven router: the first time you log in to your fresh router, you are taken straight here to change the Haven user's credentials and, optionally, create administrators.
