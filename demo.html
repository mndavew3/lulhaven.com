    Recover root access (advanced)

    Forgot your password and your spouse forgot too? This unlocks the underlying recovery account so you can set a new administrator password without factory-resetting the router.

    Root login is normally locked.

    How this works on a real Haven router: the first time you log in to your fresh router, you are taken straight here to create your first administrator. Once you do, the underlying Linux root account is locked — from then on, you and any administrator you add are the only people with access. We never use Linux jargon: it is your router, with your name on it.
